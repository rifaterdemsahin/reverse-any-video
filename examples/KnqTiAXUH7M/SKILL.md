# Style blueprint: How to reverse engineer any editing style (Turned it into a skill)

Measured from Taha El Harti | Making AI Useful's video (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KnqTiAXUH7M). Every number below came from reverse_video.py; nothing is guessed.

## Editing rhythm

- Average shot: **3.0 s** (median 2.28 s, range 0.47–15.1 s)
- **19.8 cuts per minute** across 102 shots
- First cut at 6.7 s
- Total silence: 6.2 s of 306 s (low numbers mean pauses were cut out)
- Fastest cutting: 0:00–0:30 (14 cuts in 30 s)

## Recreate it

1. Write a hook that lands in the same time window and uses the same hook type.
2. Follow the section order above; keep each section's share of the runtime.
3. Speak at the measured words-per-minute; cut pauses down to the measured silence level.
4. Cut at the measured average shot length; speed up where the original's fastest window sits.
5. Place open loops and calls to action at the same relative timestamps.
